What is a Risk to Reward Calculator?

A Risk to Reward Calculator shows the ratio between what you risk on a trade and what you stand to gain. If you risk $50 to make $100, your ratio is 1:2. A ratio of 1:2 or better means your winners can pay for your losers, even if you win less than half of your trades.

How to Use?

1. Select your asset. 2. Enter your lot size, entry price, stop loss and take profit. 3. Click Calculate Now to see your risk in pips and money, your reward in pips and money, and your risk to reward ratio in your currency.

Formula

Risk in Pips = |Entry − Stop Loss| ÷ Pip Size. Reward in Pips = |Take Profit − Entry| ÷ Pip Size. Risk : Reward = Reward in Pips ÷ Risk in Pips. Risk in USD = Pip Value × Lot Size × Risk in Pips. Reward in USD = Pip Value × Lot Size × Reward in Pips. Pip value follows the same rules as the Lot Size Calculator: forex pairs use a contract of 100,000, Gold and Silver use 100, Bitcoin uses 1, and JPY pairs divide by the entry rate.
