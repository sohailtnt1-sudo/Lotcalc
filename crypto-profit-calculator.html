What is a Crypto Profit Calculator?

A Crypto Profit Calculator shows, before you sell, exactly how much you will make or lose on a spot trade after exchange fees. It shows your investment, gross profit, total fees, net profit and return on investment, so you can see whether a trade is really worth it once fees are included.

How to Use?

1. Select your crypto. 2. Enter your buy price, sell price, quantity in coins and the buy and sell fees in percent. 3. Click Calculate Now to see investment, gross profit, total fees, net profit and ROI in your currency.

Formula

Investment = Buy Price × Quantity. Gross Profit = (Sell Price − Buy Price) × Quantity. Buy Fee = Investment × Buy Fee %. Sell Fee = (Sell Price × Quantity) × Sell Fee %. Net Profit = Gross Profit − Buy Fee − Sell Fee. ROI % = Net Profit ÷ Investment × 100.
