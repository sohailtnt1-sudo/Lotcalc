What is a Crypto Leverage & Liquidation Calculator?

A Crypto Leverage & Liquidation Calculator shows, before you open a futures trade, how much margin you need, how big your position is, and the exact price at which your position will be liquidated. With high leverage, even a small price move against you can close your position, so always know your liquidation price first.

How to Use?

1. Select your crypto and side (Long or Short). 2. Enter your entry price, quantity in coins, leverage and maintenance margin rate. 3. Click Calculate Now to see position value, margin required, liquidation price and how far it is from your entry.

Formula

Position Value = Quantity × Entry Price. Margin Required = Position Value ÷ Leverage. Long Liquidation Price = Entry × (1 − 1 ÷ Leverage + Maintenance Margin Rate). Short Liquidation Price = Entry × (1 + 1 ÷ Leverage − Maintenance Margin Rate). Distance to liquidation = |Entry − Liquidation Price| ÷ Entry × 100.
